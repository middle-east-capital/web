Who We Are

A regional advisory partner built on trust and execution.

MiddleEast Capital Advisors is an independent corporate finance firm connecting businesses in Türkiye and the Middle East with the capital, structure and strategic partners they need to grow.

We provide company-specific solutions that strengthen balance sheets, protect value in difficult times and open doors to international investors, while supporting employment and the development of the economies we work in.

Vision

To be the most trusted bridge between regional businesses and global capital.

Mission

To deliver independent, practical advice that creates measurable value for all stakeholders.

Integrity

Ethical, transparent and fully aligned with our clients' long-term interests.

Expertise

Solution-oriented, creative and competent teams with deep local knowledge.

What We Do

Advisory across the full capital lifecycle

From raising growth capital to navigating restructurings and cross-border transactions, we stand beside our clients at every decisive moment.

01

Financing Arrangement

Structuring and arranging project, acquisition and working-capital finance through local banks, GCC lenders and international institutions, including syndicated and refinancing solutions.

02

Financial & Operational Restructuring

Independent business reviews, liquidity planning and creditor negotiations that stabilise companies under pressure and restore sustainable capital structures.

03

M&A / IPO Advisory

Buy-side and sell-side mandates, minority stake sales, valuations and IPO readiness, with access to strategic and financial investors across the Gulf and Europe.

04

Commercial Real Estate Advisory

Feasibility studies, investment structuring and capital raising for commercial, hospitality and mixed-use developments, including real-estate fund solutions.

05

Family Business Sustainability

Governance, succession planning and shareholder agreements that help family-owned groups preserve wealth and stay competitive across generations.

06

Alternative Investment Platform

Curated co-investment, private credit and real-asset opportunities for institutional and family-office investors seeking exposure to the region.
